What is SmartInventory?

SmartInventory is a carefully designed inventory and warehouse management system created specifically for small and medium-sized businesses that want accurate stock control, streamlined operations and clear business visibility without unnecessary complexity.

Whether you run a retail shop, wholesale business, small manufacturing unit or multiple locations — SmartInventory helps you know exactly what you have, where it is, and how it's performing.

Core Capabilities

📦

Real-time Stock Control

Always know current stock levels across all products and locations with automatic low-stock alerts.

🔄

Complete Purchase & Sales Flow

From supplier purchase orders through goods receiving to customer sales, invoicing and delivery — fully tracked.

🏬

Multi-Location Support

Manage inventory across multiple warehouses or shops with easy stock transfers and location-specific reporting.

📊

Meaningful Business Insights

Clear visual reports showing your best sellers, slow-moving items, profit margins and stock value trends.

Role-based Access
