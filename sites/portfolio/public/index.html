About me

Full-stack developer who builds systems that work in the real world

I'm Benjamin Amoako — known as ProfJero — a full-stack developer and IT trainer based in Cape Coast, Ghana. For 5 years I have been turning ideas into reliable digital products: websites, e-learning platforms, admin dashboards, point-of-sale and inventory systems.

On the front end I craft accessible, responsive interfaces. On the back end I design databases, authentication, REST APIs and serverless functions with Node.js, PHP, Firebase and SQL — so you get one person who understands the whole system end to end.

I am the Co-Founder and Executive Director of Digital Bridge Initiative and the Founder of ProfJero Hub, where our studios combine technology, creativity, nutrition and agriculture to create impact. I love teaching, and I'm always open to collaborations that move people and businesses forward.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kind of projects do you build?

Websites, web applications, admin dashboards, e-learning platforms, inventory and point-of-sale systems, and APIs. I handle both the front end and the back end, so I can deliver a complete system.

Which technologies do you use?

HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React on the front end; Node.js, PHP, Firebase/Firestore, MySQL and REST APIs on the back end; Cloudflare, Cloudinary and Google Cloud for hosting and media. I choose the stack that fits your budget and goals.

How long does a project take?

A landing page usually takes 1–2 weeks. A business system with an admin dashboard typically takes 4–8 weeks depending on features. You get a clear timeline before we start.

Can I try your systems before hiring you?

Yes. Most projects in my portfolio have live demos and video walkthroughs on YouTube or TikTok, so you can see exactly how they work.

Do you work with clients outside Ghana?

Yes. I work remotely with clients anywhere and communicate via WhatsApp, email and video calls.

Do you offer training?

Yes. I train individuals, schools and organisations in web development, digital skills and IT, both in person and online.
